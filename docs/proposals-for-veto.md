# Proposals for the user's veto (design addenda 2.2 and 2.3)

Every item marked "proposal" while addenda 2.2 and 2.3 were folded into the books. Each goes in as
written unless the user vetoes it. Veto by number. Decision log: DA-1 to DA-16, DC-1 to DC-16.

## From addendum 2.2
1. Seven personal quest seeds, one each for Hild, Oddi, Bran, Runa, Eithne, Thrand and Áine (Book 5 sections 02 and 11).
2. Twist 1: Thorkel's debt. Thorkel bargained with Rán before the wreck, and part of the price is still owed (Book 4 section 12).
3. Twist 2: the outlaw prince. Faolán is Fiachra's outlawed eldest son, and holds the Frankish sword (Book 4 section 12; Book 5 section 14).
4. Twist 3: the barrow's silver. Hrolf the Barrow-King is Ottar's grandfather, buried with silver stolen from Kilbrecc.
5. Twist 4: the abbot's calm water. The Bell's peace was bought with an old bargain, and the abbot knows. It is left ambiguous.
6. Twist 5: the friendly pedlar sells news to everyone, Orm included.
7. Twist 6: Ylva's last fetch. Her sightings come true, then she sees the steerer's fetch.
8. Twist 7: the hostage who will not go home. Donnchad refuses to return to Ardcarn.
9. Twist 8: the grateful survivor. Someone spared in an early raid returns at the worst moment.
10. Residents' signature colours beyond the addendum: Bran in bleached linen, Runa in ochre, Áine in weld-green (Book 5; Book 7).
11. The four wandering characters' values and stories: the poet, the pedlar, the hermit and the disgraced warrior (Book 5 section 13).
12. The pedlar is the same person as the visiting Gaelic pedlar (Book 5 section 13; Book 10).
13. A death in a duel fought by its rules writes `KILLING`, but brings no guards and no feud (Book 6 section 14).
14. Taking the barrow mail or the bog torc writes `SACRED_TAKEN` (Book 6 section 13).
15. Refusing the hill-folk's Samhain call writes `PROMISE_BROKEN` (Book 6).
16. Cross-faction ties: Faolán and Fiachra; Faolán and Órlaith; Hrolf and Ottar; Hild and Kolgrím; Áine and Gilla Muire; Senán and Colmán; the pedlar and Orm (Book 5 relationship web).
17. Áine's father: the killer is now a gentle father in Saltvik, and the killing was ordered by Gilla Muire (Book 6).
18. Hild and Kolgrím: the hólmganga in the Return is the lawful way to vengeance. If Kolgrím is killed by stealth, his men fight to the end (Book 6).

## From addendum 2.3
19. Route encounters: wanderers on about half of all crossings; hostile encounters on 1 in 5 by day and 1 in 3 at night, with modifiers (Book 4 section 03; Book 10 section 14).
20. Waystations: a safe camp with a small store at one camp spot per route (6 timber, 2 stone, 4 worker-days; night risk x0.25) (Books 2, 4, 7, 9, 10).
21. The waymarked route modifier: no getting turned round in fog or at night; hostile chance x0.9 (Book 10 section 14).
22. The nine story gates: launch, Carrick raid, reply to Asgrim, defence of Skarvholm, household council, move ashore, Gathering, Return, final battle (Book 6 section 15; Book 10 section 22).
23. The defence of Skarvholm as a gate: the warning comes, and the attack waits until the player chooses the night, with pressure rising meanwhile (Book 6 scenario 8).
24. The beacon brings Orm's typical date 5 days earlier and raises the pressure faster, but the final battle still waits at its gate (Book 10 sections 07 and 10).
25. Six new deed tags: `KILLING_HIDDEN`, `HOSPITALITY_BROKEN`, `BLOOD_PRICE_PAID`, `BLOOD_PRICE_TAKEN`, `RITES_DENIED` and `CHALLENGE_REFUSED`, with what writes each (Book 6 section 01).
26. The friendship deltas for those six tags. For example, a hidden killing is Word -3; a refused challenge is Glory -3 (Book 10 section 08).
27. The culture weights for those six tags (Book 10, "Deed reactions by culture").
28. Raiding Kilbrecc after a night in its guesthouse is `HOSPITALITY_BROKEN` (Book 6 scenario 5).
29. Scripted deaths: Rán's debt, where Gudrun offers herself (Books 4 and 5).
30. Scripted deaths: the Return's cairn door. Whoever holds it dies, unless the spring-room passage was found.
31. Scripted deaths: Bran's reckoning. A monk's family demands his life as blood-price.
32. Scripted deaths: Thrand's proof. Without the evidence he dies in the duel, at the reply to Asgrim or wherever he next meets Asgrim.
33. Scripted deaths: the defence of Skarvholm. The watch leader may die holding the landing.
34. Scripted deaths: a berserker such as Kári may die charging alone.
35. Scripted deaths: the washer at the ford names who will die, and changing orders can save them at a cost.
36. Crew deaths come mainly from being left behind, from berserkers and cruel enemies, and from scripted deaths; *Rán's mercy* still prevents unscripted deaths (Book 1).
37. Ulf and Kolgrím are the named cruel enemies who finish off the downed, after a warning (Book 5).
38. A left-behind table by enemy, saying who captures and who kills (Book 6 section 16; Book 10 section 06).
39. The heavy build for Bran, Kolbeinn and Tófi (Book 5).
40. The heavy build for Kolgrím, some grey-cloaks, Fiachra's champion and berserkers, and about one in six generated people (Books 5 and 10).
41. Code stances for 14 characters: Gudrun, Runa, Kári, Tófi, Bran, Oddi, Eithne, Thrand, Senán, Muirenn, Colmán, Ottar, Orm and Heid (Book 5 section 16).
42. Hild and Áine are conventional, and so is every other major character by default (Book 5).
43. Nessa's code stance follows her hidden stance: conventional as Watcher, transgressive as Bound or Runaway (Book 5).
44. Oddi is transgressive: he steals from his own (Book 5).
45. Kári is "conventional, to the hilt": he lives the drengr ideal (Book 5).
46. Traits, likes, dislikes and a tendency for all 17 residents (Book 5 section 17).
47. Kári has the Berserk trait (Book 5 section 17).
48. Seven starting relationships: Tófi mentors Kári; Bran and Kári are rivals; Bran mentors Oddi; Hild and Oddi are friends; Nessa and Ylva are friends; Eithne and Runa are rivals; Tófi and Kolbeinn are drinking companions (Book 5).
49. The four wandering characters count as major characters, with full conversation depth (Book 5 section 18).
50. Three new consequence chains: a challenge refused, a killing hidden and then found out, and a resident left behind (Book 6 section 11).
51. Someone spared in an early raid returns (the consequence-chain form of twist 8) (Book 6 section 11).
52. Ylva's secret: her last fetch (Book 5 section 02).
53. Optional quests that need a particular companion: Runa to judge a mast timber, Senán to read a carved stone (Books 4, 5 and 6).
54. Points of interest: 1 or 2 major per site, 1 per route, and 4 to 8 minor ones (Book 10 section 20).
55. The re-cut plan: 38 stages, with the routes in new Stages 29 and 30, and the build gates at 12, 20, 24 and 33 (Book 3).
